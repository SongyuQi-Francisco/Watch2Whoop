{{APP_NAME}} is your body's data layer: it turns Apple Watch and Health data into recovery, sleep and strain you can read at a glance, and keeps your records in your own account. You decide which AI, if any, gets to read them.

DAILY OVERVIEW
- The Today page shows three rings side by side: sleep score, recovery and yesterday's strain
- Heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, wrist temperature and more, compared with your own baseline so you see the direction of change
- Stress, body battery, body age and a training load target
- 7-day, 30-day, 90-day and 365-day trends, with a comparison to the previous period
- Weekly and monthly reports

SLEEP
- Sleep stages, sleep score and bedtime for every night
- A bedtime goal: set one and compare it with your last 7 nights

LOG BY TALKING, NOT BY FORM
- Training: muscle load map, set-by-set logging for today's plan, movement limits, training calendar
- Food: quick meals, recipes, shopping list; also water, weight, journal, supplements and lab trends
- View and log offline; everything syncs when you are back online

JARVIS: YOUR AI, OPTIONAL
- Chat, voice, photos and quick logging
- Bring your own AI: your own API key, or connect Claude, ChatGPT and other clients through MCP; or use the backup AI the app provides (you are asked separately before the first use)
- AI is off by default; data is sent to the AI you chose only when you start using Jarvis
- Anything that deletes or undoes something is confirmed with you first
- Answers are AI-generated and may be wrong

CIRCLES
- Create circles with friends: workout feed, likes, comments and a leaderboard
- You choose what you share and you can leave at any time

YOUR DATA
- Recovery, sleep, strain and other scores are calculated on your iPhone
- Sync center: one-tap sync, a one-sentence reason when something is stuck, and history backfill
- Delete your account and all its data inside the app at any time
- No ads, no tracking, no selling of data

GOOD TO KNOW
Requires an iPhone with iOS 16 or later and permission to read Health data; an Apple Watch gives you fuller data. Sign in with Apple, an e-mail code or a setup QR code.
{{APP_NAME}} is not a medical device. Its scores and trends are for understanding your everyday condition and are not for diagnosing, treating or preventing any disease; if you feel unwell, talk to a doctor.
