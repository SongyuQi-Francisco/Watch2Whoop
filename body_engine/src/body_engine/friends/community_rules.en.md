# Circle community rules

Version: 2026-10-31

Circles are where you and people you know cheer each other on. Before you create or join a circle or write a comment for
the first time, please read and accept these rules.

## 1. Content that is not allowed

In comments, names, pictures and circle names, do not post:

- harassment, insults, threats or attacks on a person;
- hate speech: attacking people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, illness or similar;
- sexual, explicit or sexually suggestive content;
- violence, or content encouraging self-harm or suicide;
- spam, advertising, scams, or pretending to be someone else;
- other people's private information or health data without their consent;
- anything illegal.

## 2. What you can do

- **Block**: long-press a comment or a post, or open a member's profile, and choose "Block". You then no longer see each
  other's posts, comments, likes, member cards or leaderboard rows in circles, no longer get each other's notifications or
  cheers, and circle sharing between the two of you is paused. Undo it under "My circles → Blocked".
- **Report**: posts, comments and member profiles (name, picture) can be reported, with a reason. A report hides the item
  for you at once; an item reported by several people is hidden for everyone until an admin has looked at it.
- Harmful words are stopped before they are posted; please change the text and send it again.

## 3. What happens when the rules are broken

An admin usually handles reports within 24 hours: keeps the content, removes it, or bars the person from circles (they
are taken out of every circle). Accounts with serious or repeated violations may be disabled.

## 4. Contact

If you disagree with a decision, tell us in the app under "Me → Feedback".
